会议纪要

Meeting Summary
思路：会议／对谈纪要 = 通知、邀请信的「过去式」。通知／邀请信描述将来要发生的事，纪要描述已经发生的会议的信息点。
  1. 第一段：时间、地点、大体内容、参加人员
  2. 第二段：① 会议开始时间与开场活动 → ② 流程一 → ③ 流程二 → ④ 流程三
  3. 第三段：会议结束 + 备份
示例题干：Suppose you have attended a meeting held by your student union to welcome some international students. Please write a summary about this meeting.
Meeting Summary

On January 1, 2025 时间, our Student Union 主办方 organized a meeting for 填写目的或对象. It took place at 地点.

The meeting started at 9 a.m 时间, and the president of our union 主办方 delivered a short speech to extend our warm welcome to 对象. Then, the head of the activity department gave a presentation, introducing the campus, the main facilities, and various activities. After that, all the participants 做具体的事情. At the end of the meeting, the Student Union 主办方 summarized all items and decisions.

The meeting was adjourned at 11 a.m. as scheduled, and the meeting had been recorded and the link was shared via our website/Wechat group.

Submitted by: ______
Date: ______
