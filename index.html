段落结构模板

第一段描述 · 第二段论述 · 第三段总结
第一段 · 描述图片 / 现象
开头句
As is subtly mirrored in the image, 图画描述.
描述要素
人物 / 动物 / 事物 + 动作 + 环境（图画）　或　主题 + 对象 + 时间
占比表达
account for（占……比例）
描述现象
What the diagram conveys goes far beyond merely ______. Instead, it ______.
过渡句
Several factors weigh heavily for this round of economic recession, among which two major ones suspended on the top of the list which should never be overlooked.
饼状图 · 完整例句
As is subtly mirrored in the pie chart, factors, which influenced consumer's choices when going to the restaurants, varied a lot.
Based on the diagram given, people who choose the restaurants with the preference of special features take a comparatively large share, accounting for 37%, followed by those who prefer the service, a proportion of 33%. By contrast, the percentage of consumers who put emphasis on environment, price and other accounts for 9%, 6% and 15% respectively.
第二段 · 论述（论点 + 论据）
论点句1
In the first place, ______
论据1
Based on a most recent study, a growing body of evidence showed that most successful social elites, who ______, hold themselves accountable.
论点2
Meanwhile, 观点句2
As a consequence, the group leader needs to spend more time and energy on group affairs than others.
论据2
A classic case in point is one of my classmates, who ______.
小结句
Thus, from this standing point, there is nothing more beneficial and challenging than ______. (detrimental and harmful)
第三段 · 总结
In view of the arguments above, we can safely draw a conclusion that with the ever-increasing material and cultural life, the number of university students touring will keep growing in the forthcoming decade.
💡 其中 「论点句 + 论据」与「小结句」的句式可以互相替换使用。

十条拓宽思路的点

写论点时套用 · 十大角度
1理论联系实际
when visiting places full of knowledge and culture, our horizon can be broadened, as practical experience can be a great supplement for our book knowledge.
2经济随着中国经济的崛起
owing to the prosperity of the economy in China, the whole country promotes the policy of higher education.
3效率
due to the busy schedule of university life, college students, who are engaged in studying, must find it helpful for them to improve efficiency when reading on the smart phone.
4安全
doing sth provides a safer environment for both your life and your career.
5健康减少压力
Due to the busy schedule of university life, college students who are engaged in studying, dating and participating in other activities, are especially susceptible to omnipresent stress. (论点2) Via (by) travelling, their long standing pressure can be effectively reduced.
6交流
During/when modeling the educational behavior, the communication between generations can be enhanced.
7情感
emotional factors do play an important role in cultivating children's behavior. When doing sth, the positive emotion, which is helpful for education, can be triggered.
8环保
as the increasing awareness of resources, more and more people start to realize that it is more environmentally friendly to use the express service.
9乐趣
our interests can be satisfied during advanced education.
10国家 → 社会
从国家、社会层面切入，拔高立意（图中未给出固定英文句，写作时可按主题自行补充）。
